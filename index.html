How It Works

The prototype demonstrates the complete single-pass reconstruction workflow from video input to a 3D scene preview.
1

Video Analysis

The uploaded drone video is read and its duration and frames are examined.

2

Frame Selection

Useful frames are sampled from the single flight video while avoiding unnecessary frames.

3

Camera Motion & Depth

Visual information across frames is used to create a depth-aware spatial representation.

4

3D Reconstruction

The processed visual points are projected into a 3D-style scene for inspection.

Prototype scope: this browser demo shows the reconstruction pipeline visually. A production implementation would connect the same stages to photogrammetry tools such as COLMAP/Open3D for metric-accurate 3D reconstruction and georeferencing.
